# Help shape Corion

**Your Privacy is our main concern**

Corion is a Windows browser with a live homepage editor, split view and study
tools. We are looking for people who enjoy arranging their workspace or who keep
multiple sources open while studying.

## Try one real task

Spend about ten minutes on something you already do: arrange a homepage for a
subject, compare two articles, or keep a reference page beside a lesson. Keep your
usual browser available while you explore this early preview.

**[Start the ten-minute guide](GET_STARTED.md)** ·
**[Download the Windows preview](https://github.com/hithaixo-max/Corion-Browser/releases/download/v3.23.0/Corion-3.23.0-win-x64-setup.exe)**

Windows 10 build 19041+ / Windows 11, x64. The public installer is 3.23 and the
preview download is free. The local 3.24 Shield startup correction has not been
published in an installer yet. Read the [known limits](TROUBLESHOOTING.md).

## Share what happened

**[Send first impressions](https://github.com/hithaixo-max/Corion-Browser/issues/new?template=first_impressions.yml)**
or [talk about your setup in Discussions](https://github.com/hithaixo-max/Corion-Browser/discussions).

Tell us the version you used, what you tried, one useful detail and one problem.
If you return to Corion later, add a comment saying which feature brought you
back. A specific problem is useful even if you decide to keep another browser.

There is no registration list, email collection or required survey before you
download. Feedback through GitHub is voluntary and public, using your GitHub
account. We do not promise rewards, privileged access or a feature delivery date.

## Make your setup useful to someone else

You can share a screenshot of a layout you made and explain how you use it. Remove
personal shortcuts, account names and other private information first. Include
credits for any artwork visible in it; see [Asset credits](ASSET_CREDITS.md).

Corion is an early preview, not an independently audited privacy product. Private
tabs do not make you anonymous. No speed, security or download-count benchmark is
being claimed here.
