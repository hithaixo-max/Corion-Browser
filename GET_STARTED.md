# Your first ten minutes with Corion

Corion is an early Windows browser from Coritex Studio. Try its live homepage
editor and split view, then tell us what helped and what got in your way.
No Corion account is required to use the browser.

**Public download: 3.24 - Shield Startup Fix.** This early preview includes the
first-document filtering correction; compatibility limits still apply.

## 1. Install the preview

1. Use Windows 10 build 19041 or later, or Windows 11, on an x64 PC.
2. Download the [Windows installer](https://github.com/hithaixo-max/Corion-Browser/releases/download/v3.24.0/Corion-3.24.0-win-x64-setup.exe).
   The preview download is free. The separate troubleshooter ZIP is not the browser.
3. Read the [installation guide](INSTALLATION.md), including prerequisites,
   checksums and the unsigned-preview notice. Follow your device's software policy.
4. Close existing Corion windows before installing or upgrading, then open Corion
   from the shortcut you chose during setup.

Keep your current browser available. This preview has bugs and incomplete website
compatibility; trying Corion does not require making it your default browser.

## 2. Make the homepage useful

Open **Settings → Corion Mods** and enter the homepage editor. Move the clock,
adjust the layout and try a local background. Press **Save** to keep the result or
**Cancel** to return to your previous layout. Add a couple of shortcuts or a folder
for a subject you study.

![The live homepage editor](assets/mods-editor.png)

## 3. Put two sources beside one another

Open two ordinary web pages. Use the split-view control or a tab's **Open in split
view** context action. Drag the divider and see whether the extra space helps you
compare sources or keep a lesson beside reference material.

![Two sources in Corion split view](assets/split-view.png)

## 4. Check the separate private space

Open a private tab and look for its distinct homepage and private indicators.
Normal-profile saved logins do not load there. Private browsing does not make
your traffic anonymous; see [Privacy](PRIVACY.md).

## 5. Tell us one useful thing

[Send first impressions](https://github.com/hithaixo-max/Corion-Browser/issues/new?template=first_impressions.yml)
or [report a reproducible bug](https://github.com/hithaixo-max/Corion-Browser/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml).
GitHub requires its own account for those forms; the browser does not.

- What did you try?
- What was useful?
- What would stop you using Corion again?

The forms are public. Review screenshots before uploading them and leave out
passwords, account details, cookies, tokens and complete browser profiles.

## Known limits before you try it

- Version 3.24 corrects initial-document Shield setup and refreshes bundled lists.
  Live third-party ad blocking has not been verified for this correction; no
  blocker is guaranteed to catch every ad or tracker.
- Full Spotify playback and some protected video are unavailable in the shipped
  engine. Extension compatibility is incomplete.
- The Windows taskbar Jump List has a known unresolved issue.

See [Troubleshooting](TROUBLESHOOTING.md) for details. Screenshots retain the
photograph and other notices in [Asset credits](ASSET_CREDITS.md).
