## Short launch post

> Corion 3.23 Public Preview is available for Windows 10 / 11 x64. Make the homepage your own, work in split view, keep study notes close, and pop out supported video. Installer, diagnostics and known limits: https://hithaixo-max.github.io/Corion-Browser/

## Longer announcement

> We’re sharing Corion 3.23 — Public Preview, a personal Windows browser from Coritex Studio.
>
> Corion Mods lets you move and restyle the homepage, from clock faces and folders to local backgrounds and shortcut artwork. Split view, page-linked notes, saved study sessions, reminders and a calculator keep useful tools near your tabs. Supported video can move into a floating window while you work.
>
> Normal profiles keep their own accounts and sign-ins. Private tabs use separate temporary sessions, and Corion Shield provides per-site blocking controls. Private browsing does not make you anonymous.
>
> This is an early preview. Expect bugs and website or extension compatibility gaps. Full Spotify playback is unavailable in the shipped engine; an explicit Edge fallback is provided. The Windows Jump List also has a known unresolved issue.
>
> Download the Windows x64 installer, separate troubleshooter, trailer and documentation at https://hithaixo-max.github.io/Corion-Browser/. Clear bug reports and workflow suggestions are welcome on GitHub.
