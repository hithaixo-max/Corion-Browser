A PERSONAL PLACE ON THE WEB

01 / WHY WE MADE IT

The browser is where
your day comes together.

A lesson in one tab. Your notes in another. A collection of sources, a deadline, a song in the background. We made Corion to give those parts of your day a more personal home.

We wanted customization that goes beyond a color picker, useful study tools close to hand, and privacy controls you can find and understand. Corion brings those ideas into a dark, cave-inspired browser with an identity of its own.

We are building it step by step. It is still an early browser, and feedback helps us find the rough edges that matter.

02 / YOUR WORKSPACE

Familiar where it matters.
Personal where you want it.

Tools for study, creative work and everyday browsing.

01

Make it yours

Corion Mods is a live canvas for your homepage. Move and resize your clock, logo, shortcuts and folders. Preview your design before you save it.

  • Custom images, GIFs and supported video backgrounds
  • Clock faces, shortcut covers, toolbar icons and tab artwork
  • Optional ambient music and named appearance presets
02

Keep your place

Give different tasks their own space, then move between them without losing the thread.

  • Horizontal or optional vertical tabs, pinning and folders
  • Split view, website side panels and tabs across windows
  • Saved study sessions, source notes and tab previews
03

Stay with the lesson

Supported videos can follow you in resizable floating windows. Compact controls sit over the picture, and fullscreen gives the video your whole screen.

  • Play, pause, available next-video controls and vertical volume
  • Original playback source, without a second media player
  • Native reminders and a standard/scientific calculator
04

Choose your boundaries

Privacy controls belong in everyday browsing. Corion gives them a visible place in the workspace.

  • Corion Shield with per-site Hunting / Relaxing controls
  • Separate profiles and isolated private-tab sessions
  • Website permissions, saved logins and history controls

03 / PRIVACY, IN PLAIN LANGUAGE

A priority.
And a responsibility.

Your Privacy is our main concern

We want you to understand what your browser is doing. Your profiles and personal libraries live on your Windows computer. Private tabs use separate temporary sessions, and saved normal-profile logins do not load into them.

Websites and optional online features still make network requests. Searches go to your chosen provider; online suggestions can send typed text when enabled. Optional music recognition sends a microphone sample after you choose it and grant permission.

Private browsing is not anonymity, and no blocker catches everything. Corion is an early build that still needs continued compatibility and security work.

Does every website, extension and video work?

Compatibility is still improving. Protected content and media codecs can limit playback and pop-outs. Full Spotify web playback is currently unsupported inside Corion's shipped engine; Corion offers an explicit Microsoft Edge fallback. Extensions also have compatibility limits.

Will the original YouTube video stop using resources?

The original media still decodes and supplies audio for the floating window. Supported capture can replace its visible picture with a black placeholder. This does not remove decoding cost. Capture that depends on the original visible surface keeps that surface available.

Is Corion open source?

Corion's original browser code and branding are proprietary. This public repository contains the product website and documentation. All rights are reserved for original Corion material; third-party components retain their own licenses and notices.

What other limitations should I know about?

This is an early build: expect bugs. The Windows taskbar Jump List has an unresolved issue. Reminder alerts run while Corion is open and check missed items when you return. Optional music recognition uses an unofficial community integration.
