4.0 · AVAILABLE NOW

Finishing Touches

An early Windows x64 release. Download the installer from the official release; the publisher signature is not verified.

  • Command palette and unified local library.
  • Named workspaces and offline article reading.
  • Explicit page and selected-text translation.
  • Performance modes, reduced motion and cleaner tool windows.
  • Bounded bookmark menu work and dormant background restoration.

More in this release

A measured health dashboard, guided setup, selective backups with recovery into a separate profile, consolidated site controls and native accessibility improvements. Automatic-update controls are included, while live automatic delivery remains disabled pending production hosting and signing.

Automatic update delivery requires configured release hosting and publisher trust. The installer is unsigned, and final publisher/legal policy review remains ongoing. No worldwide legal-compliance guarantee is made.
